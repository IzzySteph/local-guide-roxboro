Raising a Family in

Roxboro, NC

A local parent's guide to the parks, playgroups, and family-friendly places in Person County.

Welcome Home!

My partner and I bought our house in Roxboro eight years ago, right before the pandemic shut everything down, and at the time I didn't know a thing about the town. Now we're raising our 3-year-old daughter here, with a baby on the way, so this guide is the one I wish I'd had when we moved in.

What makes Roxboro worth a look

Downtown looked like a ghost town when we arrived, since a lot of businesses didn't make it through the pandemic, but over the last few years new ones have been opening and bringing it back to life. We went from one coffee shop within thirty miles to about five, and every one of them is locally owned. After I became a stay-at-home mom I needed reasons to get out of the house, so I started walking with a friend and her baby from the Kirby Theater to the library and back, usually with a coffee (and sometimes breakfast) along the way. That stretch of downtown is stroller friendly, and it's still one of my favorite parts of living here.

A typical week for families

  • Playgroups: Person County Partnership for Children hosts weekly themed playgroups for parents and kids ages 0-5.
    Every Friday from 10 AM to 11:30 AM @ 111 S Main St, Roxboro, NC 27573.
  • Storytime and crafts: The library runs a storytime with a related craft every week.
    Every Monday and Thursday at 10:30 AM @ 319 S Main St, Roxboro, NC, 27573.
  • Events at Palace Pointe: There's something going on for all ages here, though it leans toward older kids and adults. They have a bowling alley, roller skating, arcade, movie theater, laser tag, ax throwing, billiards, and a resturant all under one roof!
    Visit their Facebook page for the most recent events and updates
    Located at 5050 Durham Rd., Roxboro, NC, 27574

What to know before you come

Roxboro has a great community, but it can take some effort to find it. Indoor options for little ones are limited outside the library and the weekly playgroup, and Palace Pointe is mostly open late afternoon and evening, so it's a better fit for older kids. For medical care, most families drive to Durham because local care is crowded and short on resources. Person Memorial doesn't have a maternity ward, so if you're expecting, you'll be heading to Duke Regional to give birth.
